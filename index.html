Quick Instructions & Usage Tips
Keyboard Shortcuts

Use your numeric keypad for numbers, +, -, *, / for basic operations, and Enter or = to compute. Press Esc for Clear (AC) or Backspace for Delete.

History Memory Panel

All successful calculations are saved to the sidebar history panel. Click on any past calculation in the feed to restore it directly back onto the active display screen.

Memory Options

Use key operators to store or subtract sums into local cookie states. Click "Clear History" at the bottom of the history feed to free memory blocks.
